# Nations Light — Meta Page Playbook

Step-by-step operating system for a Facebook + Instagram page celebrating Native American / Indigenous Nations cultures for Indigenous audiences and the world — using **official Meta tools only**.

## Non‑negotiables

1. **Indigenous audiences first.** Do not exoticize, romanticize, or flatten Nations into one aesthetic.
2. **Credit and consent.** Name Nations, artists, photographers, and speakers. Get permission for sacred, ceremonial, or private material — if unsure, do not post it.
3. **No fake identity.** The Page can amplify Native voices; it must not impersonate a Nation or claim enrollment it does not have.
4. **Human approval.** This agent drafts and plans. You publish after review.
5. **Meta terms.** Use Business Suite / Ads Manager / approved APIs. No unofficial “merge into account” bots, scrapers, or hourly spam.

---

## Phase 0 — Setup (Day 1)

### Step 1 — Create the business container
1. Open [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/).
2. Confirm you have a **Facebook Page** (not only a personal profile).
3. Convert Instagram to a **Professional** account and **link** it to the Page.
4. In Business settings, assign **Page roles** (Admin, Editor, Moderator). Prefer 2‑person access for continuity.

### Step 2 — Brand brief (fill once, reuse forever)
Write answers in a note you keep next to Business Suite:

| Field | Example prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Mission | Why this Page exists in one sentence |
| Primary audience | Indigenous youth / families / urban Natives / allies (pick 1–2) |
| Pillars (3–5) | Language, land, makers, food, contemporary excellence |
| Voice | Warm, clear, proud, modern — not “museum brochure” |
| Hard bans | Sacred ceremony without consent; stereotypes; fake “chief” memes; AI faces of real people |

### Step 3 — Meta tools checklist
- [ ] Business Suite → Content → calendar view works
- [ ] Instagram linked; posting from Suite reaches both where intended
- [ ] Page username + Instagram handle match or are clearly related
- [ ] About / bio includes mission + contact / link-in-bio
- [ ] Professional dashboard insights visible

## Phase 1 — Content engine (Week 1)

### Step 4 — Choose 4 content pillars
Pick four rotating themes so the feed feels coherent:

1. **Living culture** — language clips, beadwork process, recipes, music (with credit)
2. **People** — makers, athletes, educators, youth leaders (interview or quote cards)
3. **Land & stewardship** — place-based stories, conservation, seasonal calendars
4. **Today** — news wins, book drops, events, student art, community calls

### Step 5 — Sustainable cadence (not hourly)
Target that draws attention **without** burning trust:

| Format | Cadence | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reels (IG + FB) | 4–6 / week | Discovery & follows |
| Feed posts | 3–5 / week | Depth & saves |
| Stories | Daily when possible | Habit & community |
| Live | 1–2 / month | Trust & revenue moments |
| Collabs | 2–4 / month | Cross-audience growth |

**Rule:** One strong Reel beats ten weak posts.

### Step 6 — Weekly batch ritual (90 minutes)
Every Sunday (or your best day):

1. Open Business Suite → Insights (last 7 days). Note top 3 posts by **saves + shares** (not vanity likes alone).
2. Draft **6 Reel hooks** and **4 captions** for the week.
3. Schedule in Business Suite (stagger times; test evenings and lunch windows).
4. Prep Story stickers: poll, quiz, “ask me,” link.
5. Queue 5 reply templates for common comments (thanks / resource / correction / spam).

## Phase 2 — Fast attention (Weeks 2–4)

### Step 7 — Hook formulas that travel
Use these for Reels openings (first 1–2 seconds):

- “Three things outsiders get wrong about ___ Nation…”
- “Watch how this artist finishes ___ in 15 seconds”
- “A word in ___ that English doesn’t have — and why it matters”
- “Before you buy ‘Native-style’ art, check this”
- “Today’s land acknowledgment → tomorrow’s action”

Always add: Nation credit, maker credit, soft CTA (“Follow for more living culture, not stereotypes”).

### Step 8 — Meta discovery levers
Inside Business Suite / Instagram:

1. **Reels cover + on-screen text** readable muted.
2. **Collaborations** — invite Native creators/Pages as Collab partners (consent first).
3. **Share to Stories** of every strong Reel same day.
4. **Boost winners only** — after 24–48h organic signal, boost the top 1–2 creatives with a small budget.
5. **Ads Manager tests** — objective: Profile visits or Engagement; 2 creatives × 2 audiences; kill losers in 48–72h.

### Step 9 — Community ops (trust = growth)
Daily 10–15 minutes:

- Reply to every good-faith comment in the first hour when you can
- Pin a comment with resources / Nation credit
- Hide spam; ban hate
- Thank sharers; ask a follow-up question to extend the thread

## Phase 3 — Followers that stick

### Step 10 — Profile conversion
Bio structure:

1. Who we serve + what we post  
2. Credibility line (partners, Nation voices featured — only if true)  
3. Clear CTA + link (Linktree / Beacons / your site)

Pinned content:

1. Welcome Reel: “What this Page is / isn’t”  
2. Best-performing story compilation  
3. How to support Native makers (buy direct, credit, no knockoffs)

### Step 11 — Growth loops that Meta rewards
- Series Reels (Part 1/2/3) so people return
- UGC prompts: “Show your beadwork / language flashcard”
- Monthly Live with a guest (recorded clips → more Reels)
- Cross-post selectively; prefer native edits per surface

**Avoid:** Giveaways that attract prize-hunters only; purchased followers; engagement pods; controversial bait.

## Phase 4 — Revenue (when ready)

### Step 12 — Eligibility path
In Meta monetization tools (names vary by region):

1. Check **in-stream / Reels Play / Stars / badges** eligibility on Page + IG
2. Turn on what’s available; complete tax / payout setup
3. Stay within Partner Monetization Policies (original content, no scraped media)

### Step 13 — Revenue stack (stack slowly)
| Stream | How |
| --- | --- |
| Meta native monetization | Eligible Reels / Stars / fan support |
| Partnership / branded content | Paid collabs with aligned brands; use branded content tools + disclosure |
| Affiliate | Tools, books, Native-owned shops — always disclose |
| Direct | Merch, prints, courses, events — link in bio |
| Ads for your own offer | Ads Manager traffic to landing page |

### Step 14 — Offer ladder
1. Free value on feed (trust)  
2. Email / community list (owned audience)  
3. Low-ticket digital (calendar, language pack, playlist)  
4. Higher-ticket (workshops, consulting, events)  
5. Sponsors that respect Indigenous partners (vet carefully)
