# CreditFix quick reference (cheat sheet)

## Score levers (typical importance)
Payments > Utilization > Age > Mix > New credit

## 30-day sprint
1. Autopay minimums everywhere
2. Utilization under 30% (stretch <10% before statement close)
3. 3-bureau inventory + classify
4. Dispute only errors with evidence
5. No new hard pulls

## Dispute vs rebuild
- Wrong / not yours / unverifiable → dispute or validate
- Accurate late/collection → on-time streak, payoff/settle, optional goodwill, time

## Hard no’s
CPNs, fake IDs, synthetic files, guaranteed deletion of accurate negatives, shotgun false disputes
