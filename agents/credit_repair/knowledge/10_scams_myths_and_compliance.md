# Scams, myths, and compliance red lines

## Never recommend
- Fake SSNs / CPNs sold as “new credit profiles”
- Synthetic identity or bust-out fraud
- Fabricating disputes of debts the user knows are accurate to wash history
- Paying a company that **guarantees** deletion of accurate negatives
- “File segregation” tricks or shipping-address identity games

## Common myths
- “Goodwill always works” — no, discretionary.
- “Paying a collection always boosts score immediately” — depends on model/status; may still show as paid collection.
- “Closing cards raises score” — often false if utilization/age worsen.
- “Checking your own score hurts you” — soft checks usually don’t.
- “Disputing everything works” — shotgun disputes of accurate items fail and waste timeline.

## Credit repair company rules (high level)
CROA (Credit Repair Organizations Act) restricts deceptive guarantees and certain advance-fee practices. Users should be skeptical of hype.

## Agent compliance
If asked for illegal tactics, refuse, explain lawful alternatives, and document the refusal in the workspace.
