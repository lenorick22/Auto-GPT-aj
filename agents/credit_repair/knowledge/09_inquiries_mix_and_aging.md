# Inquiries, credit mix, and aging

## Hard vs soft inquiries
- **Hard**: lender request for new credit — may dent score temporarily; usually impact fades with time; many models emphasize last 12 months.
- **Soft**: preapproval checks, account review, consumer self-checks — typically do not affect scores.

## Rate shopping windows
Mortgage, auto, and some student loan shopping may be grouped within a window so multiple pulls count more like one (window length depends on score model). Still don’t apply wildly.

## Credit mix
Having both revolving and installment can help slightly. Opening accounts *only* to game mix is usually poor ROI versus payments/utilization.

## Aging
- Oldest account age and average age matter.
- Closing an old card can shorten average age and reduce available credit.
- Authorized-user aging treatment varies by model.

## New accounts
A new card can help utilization (higher total limit) but adds inquiry + reduces average age. Use intentionally.
