# Score models and factors

## Common models
- **FICO Score** variants (often FICO 8; mortgage lenders may use older FICO 2/4/5 models).
- **VantageScore 3.0/4.0** used by many free score services.
- Free app scores can differ from lender scores — treat them as directional, not identical.

## Typical FICO-style emphasis (approximate)
1. **Payment history (~35%)** — on-time vs late/charge-off/collection.
2. **Amounts owed / utilization (~30%)** — revolving balances ÷ limits; installment utilization matters less for many models.
3. **Length of credit history (~15%)** — average age and age of oldest account.
4. **Credit mix (~10%)** — revolving + installment over time; do not open junk accounts only for mix.
5. **New credit (~10%)** — recent accounts and hard inquiries.

## Practical priority order for most people
1. Stop new lates (autopay at least minimums).
2. Lower revolving utilization (often under 30%, stretch under 10% before statement close).
3. Fix report **errors**.
4. Avoid unnecessary hard pulls / new accounts.
5. Protect age of file (don’t casually close oldest cards).
6. Only then optimize mix / product strategy.

## What does *not* directly appear on most scores
- Income, employment, bank balances (lenders may still underwrite these).
- Race, religion, national origin, sex, marital status (ECOA protections).
- Where you live, by itself, as a FICO input.

## Score ≠ approval
A higher score helps, but lenders also use DTI, income stability, collateral, and internal models.
