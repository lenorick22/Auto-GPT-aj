# Identity theft and fraud

## Immediate actions
1. Freeze credit at Equifax, Experian, TransUnion (and check specialty freezes if relevant).
2. Place an initial fraud alert if appropriate.
3. Report at **IdentityTheft.gov** and follow the personalized plan.
4. Change passwords; enable MFA on email/banking.
5. Review all three reports for unknown accounts/inquiries.

## Disputing fraudulent accounts
- Use identity theft affidavit + ID + proof of address.
- Dispute with bureaus and contact fraud departments of furnishers.
- Request written confirmation that fraudulent accounts are closed/removed.

## Mixed files
Sometimes another person’s data merges into yours (similar name/SSN errors). Treat as bureau data problem — provide identifying docs and request file separation.

## Synthetic identity
Fraud rings combine real and fake data. Victims may see unfamiliar tradelines. Still use freezes + official recovery paths; consider specialist attorney if severe.

## Tax / employment fraud signals
Unexpected tax transcripts, W-2s, or benefits claims require IRS/SSA and IdentityTheft.gov workflows in addition to credit freezes.
