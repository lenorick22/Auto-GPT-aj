# Utilization and payment tactics

## Utilization basics
`Utilization ≈ total revolving balances / total revolving limits` (overall and per card).
Many people aim for:
- Under **30%** overall as a baseline
- Under **10%** (sometimes even lower) before statement closing dates for score-sensitive moments

## High-impact moves
1. Autopay **at least** the minimum on every account (protect payment history).
2. Pay revolving balances down before the statement closes (reported balance often drives utilization).
3. Request credit line increases **without a hard pull** when possible (lowers utilization if balance stays flat).
4. Avoid closing old cards solely to “simplify” — can raise utilization and shorten average age.
5. If carrying debt, use snowball (motivation) or avalanche (math) — see `credit_math.py`.

## Statement-date tactic
Paying a few days before statement close can lower the balance that gets reported, even if you still spend later in the cycle. Still pay by the due date.

## “AZEO” style approaches
Some enthusiasts leave All Zero Except One small reported balance. This is optional optimization — not required, and effects vary by model.

## Installment loans
Paying down installment loans helps debt load but usually doesn’t move “utilization” the same way revolving does. Don’t refinance recklessly just for score cosmetics.

## Authorized user (AU) pits & peaks
AU on a well-managed seasoned card can help thin files; AU on a maxed/late card can hurt. Confirm the primary’s habits first.
