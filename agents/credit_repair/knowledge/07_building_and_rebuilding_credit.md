# Building and rebuilding credit

## Thin file / no file
Options often include:
- Secured credit cards (deposit-backed)
- Credit-builder loans from credit unions
- Becoming an authorized user on a responsible person’s card
- Certain rent/utility reporting services (effects vary; fees possible)

## After damage (lates/collections)
1. Clean errors.
2. Make every current account spotless on-time.
3. Shrink utilization.
4. Keep oldest positive accounts open.
5. Add new positive tradelines only if needed and affordable.
6. Give time — rebuilding is months to years, not days.

## Product ladder (illustrative)
1. Secured card or builder loan → on-time history
2. Graduating to unsecured card
3. Modest limit growth
4. Later: auto/mortgage only when fundamentals are ready

## Bankruptcies / major derogs
Focus on: re-established payment history, low utilization, no new derogs, accurate reporting. Lender overlays may be stricter than score alone (e.g., waiting periods after bankruptcy).

## Nonprofit counseling
HUD-approved housing counselors and nonprofit credit counselors can help with budgets and debt management plans (DMPs). Vet fees and nonprofit status.
