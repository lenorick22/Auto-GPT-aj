# Situational playbooks

## Applying for an apartment in ~30 days
- Pull reports now; dispute only clear errors.
- Pay cards down before statement closes.
- Prepare explanation letter for accurate negatives (honest, short).
- Avoid new hard pulls.

## Mortgage in 3–6 months
- Full 3-bureau audit immediately.
- Don’t close old cards; don’t open junk accounts.
- Heavy utilization reduction.
- Ask lender which score model/version they use.
- Large authorized-user changes late in the process can look like manipulation — be cautious.

## Auto loan soon
- Similar to mortgage but shop within inquiry grouping windows.
- Watch DTI and recent lates closely.

## Behind on cards but not yet charged off
- Contact hardship/assistance early.
- Autopay minimums if possible to prevent new lates.
- Budget triage before optimizing score cosmetics.

## Wage garnishment / lawsuit risk
- This is beyond DIY template territory — refer to a consumer attorney / legal aid quickly.
- Still freeze if fraud is involved; still document collectors’ conduct.

## Student loans
- Federal vs private rules differ (IDR, rehab, consolidation). Point users to StudentAid.gov for federal options; don’t invent discharge claims.

## Medical collections
- Confirm current reporting rules; bill errors are common — request itemized bills.
- Insurance adjustments can change balances — document thoroughly.
