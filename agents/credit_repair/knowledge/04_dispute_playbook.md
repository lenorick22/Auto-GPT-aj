# Dispute playbook

## Golden rule
Dispute **errors** and unverifiable items. Do **not** mass-dispute accurate negatives just to stall collectors — that is bad faith and often fails.

## Choose the tool
| Situation | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong balance/status/dates; not your account; duplicate; obsolete | Bureau dispute + furnisher dispute |
| Accurate late, but long on-time since then | Goodwill request (courtesy — no guarantee) |
| Unfamiliar collection / unclear ownership of debt | Debt validation to collector |
| Confirmed identity theft | Freeze + IdentityTheft.gov plan + disputes with affidavit |
| Accurate unpaid debt you owe | Payment plan / settlement / rebuild — not fake disputes |

## Evidence pack
- Government photo ID
- Proof of address
- Statements showing correct status/balance
- FTC identity theft affidavit / police report if theft
- Timeline notes

## Process hygiene
1. One clear issue set per letter when possible.
2. Send certified mail + keep copies (or use official portals and screenshot confirmations).
3. Log filed date, method, tracking, due date, outcome in `trackers/dispute_log.csv`.
4. Follow up near day 30 if no response.
5. If bureau verifies but you still have proof of error, escalate to furnisher, add evidence, consider CFPB complaint.

## Outcomes
- Deleted
- Updated/corrected
- Verified as reported (then pivot to rebuild or more evidence)
- Partial change

## What “verification” means
A furnisher may verify based on their records. If those records are wrong, stronger documentation or legal help may be needed.
