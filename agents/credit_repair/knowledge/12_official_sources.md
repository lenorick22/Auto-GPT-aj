# Official sources to re-verify

Always prefer primary sources; URLs and programs change.

## Reports & freezes
- AnnualCreditReport.com — federal legally required free reports access point
- Equifax, Experian, TransUnion freeze/dispute portals

## Government / regulators
- ConsumerFinancialProtectionBureau.gov — credit reports, scores, debt collection, complaints
- FTC.gov / IdentityTheft.gov — identity theft recovery
- USA.gov consumer credit pages

## Statutes (read summaries on CFPB/FTC first)
- FCRA
- FDCPA
- FCBA
- ECOA
- CROA

## Counseling
- HUD.gov approved housing counseling
- NFCC.org for nonprofit credit counseling directories

## Agent habit
When researching live, update `01_rights_and_sources.md` in the workspace with **today’s date** and the URLs actually visited.
