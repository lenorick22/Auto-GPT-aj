# Rights & sources (agent should refresh with dated links)

## Core rights (US)
- Free credit reports via AnnualCreditReport.com (weekly access has been available — re-verify)
- Dispute inaccurate/incomplete/unverifiable items; bureaus generally investigate within ~30 days
- Security freezes and fraud alerts with each bureau
- Identity theft recovery plan at IdentityTheft.gov
- Submit complaints to the CFPB when a company fails to respond appropriately
