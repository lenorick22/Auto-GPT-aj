# Sample credit report dispute letter (template)

Use only for information you believe is **inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable**.
Do not dispute accurate negative items solely to delay collection.

---

[Your Full Legal Name]  
[Your Current Address]  
[City, State ZIP]  
[Phone] · [Email]  

[Date]

[Credit Bureau Name]  
[Bureau Dispute Address]

**Re: Dispute of inaccurate information — [Your Name], DOB [MM/DD/YYYY]**

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to dispute the following information on my credit report. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, I request that you investigate these items and correct or delete any information that cannot be verified as accurate and complete.

**Items in dispute**

| Creditor / furnisher | Account / reference # | Why I dispute it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Name] | [Number] | [e.g. Not my account / Incorrect balance / Duplicate / Too old / Wrong status] |

Enclosed are copies of supporting documents (for example: ID, proof of address, statements, police report for identity theft). Please send written results of your investigation.

Sincerely,  

[Your Signature]  
[Your Printed Name]

**Enclosures:** copy of government ID; proof of address; [other evidence]
