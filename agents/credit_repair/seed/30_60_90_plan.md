# 30 / 60 / 90 day rebuild plan

## Days 1–30 — Stabilize & surface errors
- [ ] Pull 3-bureau reports; freeze if fraud risk  
- [ ] Complete inventory + classify every line  
- [ ] Autopay at least minimums on all open accounts  
- [ ] Cut revolving utilization under 30% (per-card and overall)  
- [ ] Mail/portal **error-only** disputes + log them  
- [ ] Validate unfamiliar collections  

## Days 31–60 — Squeeze the math
- [ ] Push utilization toward ≤10% before statement dates  
- [ ] Follow up unanswered disputes (~30 days)  
- [ ] Goodwill letters for isolated lates after sustained on-time history  
- [ ] Avoid new hard inquiries & impulsive account closures  
- [ ] Recalculate payoff order with `credit_math.py snowball|avalanche`  

## Days 61–90 — Compound & verify
- [ ] Re-pull reports; refresh inventory  
- [ ] Escalate unresolved errors (furnisher + CFPB complaint if warranted)  
- [ ] Protect on-time streak; revisit authorized-user / secured-card options carefully  
- [ ] Publish ranked `NEXT_ACTIONS.md` and calendar the next 4 weeks  

## Guardrails
Accurate negatives are not “fixed” by spam disputes. Rebuild is mostly **behavior + time**, with disputes reserved for real inaccuracies.
