# Sample goodwill adjustment letter (template)

Use when an account is **yours and accurate**, but you want the creditor to consider removing or updating a late payment as a courtesy after you have restored good standing.

---

[Your Full Legal Name]  
[Your Current Address]  
[City, State ZIP]  
[Account number]

[Date]

[Creditor / Servicer Name]  
[Customer Service / Credit Reporting Address]

**Re: Goodwill request — Account [number]**

Dear [Creditor],

I have been a customer since [year] and value this relationship. On [date(s)], my account was reported late because [brief honest reason]. Since then I have [paid as agreed / brought the account current / maintained on-time payments for X months].

I respectfully ask whether you would consider a goodwill adjustment to update or remove the late payment notation(s) from the credit bureaus as a courtesy. Doing so would help me continue rebuilding my credit while remaining a reliable customer.

Thank you for reviewing my request. I can be reached at [phone/email].

Sincerely,  

[Your Signature]  
[Your Printed Name]
