# Debt validation letter (collections)

Use when a collector contacts you and you need verification of the debt. Timing rules can matter — respond promptly.

---

[YOUR NAME]  
[ADDRESS]

[DATE]

[COLLECTOR NAME]  
[ADDRESS]

**Re: Request for validation — Reference [REF# if any]**

To Whom It May Concern:

I am requesting validation of this debt. Please provide:

1. The name of the original creditor  
2. The account number (original and current)  
3. The amount claimed, with an itemization of principal, interest, fees  
4. Proof you are licensed/authorized to collect in my state (if applicable)  
5. Documentation showing I am responsible for this debt  

Until validated, please do not report or continue reporting this item without verifying accuracy. Mail all correspondence to my address above.

Sincerely,  
[YOUR NAME]
